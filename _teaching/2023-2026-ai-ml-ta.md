Teaching assistant for the module *Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning*, academic years 2023–24, 2024–25 and 2025–26, working with [Max Little](https://www.maxlittle.net/home/index.php) and [Rickson C. Mesquita](https://rcmesquita.info/).

The module introduces the core concepts of artificial intelligence and machine learning, focusing on the underlying principles such as knowledge representation, search, decision theory, probability, and statistical learning.
