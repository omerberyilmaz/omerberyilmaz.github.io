This work presents a method for analyzing recurrent dynamical signals using topology-preserving techniques. We introduce flow-aware ellipsoidal neighborhoods for each data point using local spatio-temporal covariance, rather than traditional isotropic distance-based approaches. The research demonstrates that persistent homology, when enhanced with domain-specific knowledge about anisotropy, becomes more informative for dynamical systems analysis. Applications include topology-preserving denoising and improved recurrence-time estimation, with results validated on both synthetic data and real accelerometer recordings.
