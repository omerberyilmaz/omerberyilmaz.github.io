This paper presents a novel method for persistent homology that uses ellipsoid-shaped neighborhoods aligned with signal gradients, rather than the standard spherical (isotropic) approach. The technique determines a data-driven averaging neighborhood based on the most persistent topological feature, demonstrating superior noise reduction compared to conventional filtering approaches — particularly for lower-amplitude signal components. The method was accepted and presented at NOLTA 2025, and received the **Student Paper Award**.
